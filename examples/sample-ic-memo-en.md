# Aster Compute (ASTER) — Investment Committee Summary

**Date (UTC):** 2026-10-28 12:00 · **Price:** $0.042 · **Market cap / FDV:** $18.4M / $62.0M · **24h volume:** $1.1M
**Decision:** screen · **Horizon / numeraire:** 12 months / USD
**Investability gate:** conditional — verified token and chain; no revenue right; thin exit.
**Verdict:** watch · **Confidence:** medium-low

## Thesis — three points

1. A real niche: decentralized GPU inference at a 12% take rate, with 1,200 paid accounts and 54% gross margin.
2. Usage is growing and the product is differentiated on price and data retention.
3. If paid demand diversifies, the token could re-rate from a narrative asset to a cash-flow-adjacent asset.

## Counter-thesis — three strongest points

1. The token has no claim on revenue, governance or a contractual buyback; value capture is weak.
2. 41% of revenue comes from ten customers, two of them affiliated with the founders.
3. Emissions are 28% of float, a 6.2% unlock is 45 days away, and ±2% depth is only $180k.

## Valuation range

| Scenario | Key assumption | Per unit | Versus current |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Bear | Paid demand stalls; unlock absorbed at a discount | $0.026 | -38% |
| Base | 25% paid-demand growth; take rate holds | $0.045 | +7% |
| Bull | Paid demand >60%; enterprise contracts diversify | $0.071 | +69% |

The range is scenario-based, not a DCF: there is no audited revenue and no enforceable cash-flow claim. The current price already embeds growth; the margin of safety is negative in the bear case.

## Falsifiers — three evidence points that would reverse the decision

1. Paid demand falls below 30% for two quarters while emissions stay above 25% of float.
2. The top ten customers exceed 50% of revenue after the enterprise cohort launches.
3. The buyback is suspended or funded by new token issuance rather than operating cash flow.
