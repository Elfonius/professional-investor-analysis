# Examples

These files show the expected shape and reasoning quality of the output. They are **fictional** and the numbers are illustrative; they are not analyses of real assets and not investment advice.

| File | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `sample-screen-en.md` | One-page screen: gate, product, instrument, valuation, scorecard, verdict, limits |
| `sample-ic-memo-en.md` | One-page investment committee summary with thesis, counter-thesis, scenarios, catalysts, risks, falsifiers and monitoring |
| `engy-sn53-deep-report-tr.pdf` | Real-world deep-report output (Turkish, 14 pages, 6 charts). Data as of 2026-10-28; included as a design and structure example, not a current recommendation. |

## How to read the samples

- The screen and IC memo are deliberately **conditional**. A professional memo does not turn missing evidence into a confident call.
- The scorecard is not a composite score. It reports dimensions separately because a strong product cannot compensate for a broken instrument or an unassessable legal position.
- The deep PDF shows the production standard: cover, one-page executive summary, section narrative, charts with source/date captions, valuation range, risk matrix, monitoring dashboard and page numbers.

## Using the examples in an agent

Ask the agent to read the relevant sample only **after** it has read `skill/SKILL.md` and the matching template. The sample is a quality bar, not a source of facts. Never copy its numbers into a real analysis.
