# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where people can disagree about investments, methods and evidence without being dismissed or harassed. We pledge to make participation welcoming regardless of experience, identity, background or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Critique the argument, the evidence and the method — not the person.
- State assumptions and uncertainty; do not present a guess as a fact.
- Respect different asset classes, jurisdictions and levels of access to data.
- Give credit for sources and prior work.
- Accept correction gracefully and correct the record when you are wrong.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information, client data, credentials or non-public documents.
- Presenting promotional claims as verified facts, or using the project to promote a specific asset without disclosure.
- Plagiarism, or copying paywalled/commercial material without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or reviews.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that violate this code. They may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for serious or repeated violations.

Reports can be made through a private GitHub issue to the maintainers or, where available, GitHub's report tools. Reports will be reviewed as promptly as possible and handled with discretion. Retaliation against a reporter is not tolerated.

## Scope

This code applies in repository spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone represents the project in public.
